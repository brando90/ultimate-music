## What the arrangement is

A hand-written school-orchestra arrangement: strings carry the clean intro lines, brass and winds handle the rhythmic hits, percussion holds the long-form structure. From the blog post: Brando wrote it in Sibelius 4 at age 16 while referencing a MIDI of the song by ear.

## MIDI: not on the website

The website has **no MIDI file** of the arrangement. It has the PDF, the two `.sib` files and the MP3 playback. A MIDI needs one of: export from Sibelius (File > Export > MIDI) on Brando's Mac, or an import of the `.sib` into a notation program that reads it. Until then the MP3 playback is the audio input for Suno. When a MIDI exists, add it here as `the-day-that-never-comes.mid`.
