## What costs money?

RouteNote Free costs $0 upfront and has no monthly or annual distribution fee. Brando receives 85% of the net revenue RouteNote receives; RouteNote retains 15%. This revenue share is ongoing when revenue is earned, rather than a subscription bill.

RouteNote Premium is optional: a single costs $10 initially, followed by $9.99 per release annually. It is not the chosen plan.

RouteNote explicitly permits cover distribution to Spotify without the artist obtaining a separate mechanical license. A mechanical license concerns reproduction and distribution of the composition, distinct from rights in a particular recording. The conclusion here is limited to Spotify-only distribution; physical sales and other stores or territories can require different licensing.

## Why is this possible?

The original songwriters still get paid. Streaming services have licensing and royalty obligations for compositions. In the United States, eligible digital music services obtain blanket mechanical licenses, and the Mechanical Licensing Collective collects and distributes the corresponding royalties. Those obligations sit at the service level for covered streaming uses, so RouteNote does not require a separate artist-purchased mechanical license for Spotify.

Separately, RouteNote funds its Free distribution service through its share of recording revenue. The 15% distributor share is not a purchase of Metallica's composition rights.

## Conditions for this particular Suno jazz cover

Changing instrumentation or performing an instrumental version can still qualify as a cover. However, the United States compulsory-license arrangement privilege does not permit changing the basic melody or fundamental character of the composition. A substantially rewritten adaptation may need direct permission; a generic cover license or payment does not automatically fix that.

The recording must have appropriate source and commercial-use rights. A paid Suno plan does not grant Metallica's composition rights or permission to use Metallica's original recording. Prior project evidence records Premier coverage at creation and a source matching Brando's documented Sibelius playback. That supports provenance but is not a legal determination about all source permissions, arrangement changes, or the final file.

Suno says commercial rights for songs made while subscribed survive cancellation; a new subscription is not required merely to keep those rights. Free-plan songs do not automatically gain commercial rights through a later subscription.

RouteNote accepts some artificial-intelligence-generated music, subject to source terms, truthful disclosure and moderation. Approval is not guaranteed. Keep original songwriter credits, identify the track as a cover, and do not imply Metallica endorsed or performed the recording.
