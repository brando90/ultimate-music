### Bio (about 100 words)

Brando Miranda is a PhD student in computer science at Stanford University who works on artificial intelligence. In 2010, as a high school student, he arranged Metallica's "The Day That Never Comes" by hand for his school orchestra. In 2026 he returned to that score and used the AI music tool Suno to reimagine it as bebop jazz, with a small-ensemble sound. This release is a personal, non-commercial project made for the love of the music, and it is his first single.

## Release description

"The Day That Never Comes" began as a hand-written arrangement for a high school orchestra in 2010. Sixteen years later, Brando Miranda gave that arrangement to Suno, an AI music tool, and asked for a bebop jazz reading of it. This single is the result: a 3 minute 2 second small-ensemble jazz take on Metallica's song.

It is a cover of a Metallica composition and a personal, non-commercial release. It is not a recording of an orchestra, and the audio was generated with Suno, not recorded in a studio.
